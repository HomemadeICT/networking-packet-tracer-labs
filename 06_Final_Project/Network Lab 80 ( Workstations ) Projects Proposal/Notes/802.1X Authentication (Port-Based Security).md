### 802.1X Authentication (Port-Based Security)

This ensures that only authorized devices can "talk" to your switches. Without this, anyone could walk into the Hardware Lab, plug a rogue laptop into an Ethernet wall jack, and access your network.

- **How it works:** When a PC connects, the switch holds the port in a "restricted" state. The PC must provide credentials (via a RADIUS server). If valid, the switch opens the port.
    
- **Role in your Labs:** * **Hardware Lab:** Ideal here since students may be swapping components or testing different machines; it ensures only registered lab equipment gets an IP address.
    
    - **Dynamic VLAN Assignment:** You can configure 802.1X so that if a "Graphics Student" logs in at a "Hardware Lab" PC, the switch automatically moves that port into the **Graphics VLAN** (VLAN 30) just for that session.
